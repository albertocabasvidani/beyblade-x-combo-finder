Wyvern Hover is the defense blade that holds its own at the top of the Beyblade X meta. Most Western players know it as Hover Wyvern, the Hasbro name, with the two words swapped. It also has an unusual origin: it debuted in the Hasbro set G0842 before appearing in the Takara Tomy sets UX-00 and UX-21.

The stats are almost one-dimensional: 60 defense, 27 stamina, 13 attack. Wyvern Hover is built to absorb hits, and the tables below show how often that plan reaches the top cut.

## The best Wyvern Hover combo: 9-60 Kick

The reference build is [Hover Wyvern 9-60 Kick](/combos/hover-wyvern-9-60-kick/). The 9-60 ratchet is on more than half of Wyvern Hover's top-cut placements, and Kick is its most common bit.

Kick matters because a static defender gets surrounded: high defense alone would leave the blade parked and exposed. Kick gives it enough movement to avoid that, while the 60 defense does the work of cushioning impacts.

## Rush and Low Rush: the attacking defender

The next builds in the ranking keep 9-60 and switch to **Rush** or **Low Rush**. They turn Wyvern Hover into something closer to a counter-attacker, a heavy blade that moves fast. At the 2025 World Championship, the bronze medalist (Berguiny) played Hover Wyvern 7-60 Low Rush in his deck, a sign that this direction works at the highest level too.

## What it has to stop, and what stops it

A defender exists to survive pressure like [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/)'s: direct, repeated, high-power hits. That's Wyvern Hover's job, and 9-60 Kick is the setup most players trust for it.

Its weak side is the long game. With 27 stamina, it can't out-spin a stamina blade like [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) if the match becomes a contest of endurance. Wyvern Hover has to win the clash, not the wait.

## Where to get Wyvern Hover

It came first in the Hasbro product G0842 and then in the Takara Tomy releases UX-00 and UX-21. As with most competitive builds, the 9-60 ratchet and the Kick bit don't come in the same box.

## Quick answers

**Is it Wyvern Hover or Hover Wyvern?** The same blade: Wyvern Hover is the Takara Tomy name, Hover Wyvern the Hasbro one.

**What is the best Wyvern Hover combo?** 9-60 Kick, by a clear margin over every other build.
