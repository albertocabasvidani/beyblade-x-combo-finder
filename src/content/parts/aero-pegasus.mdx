Aero Pegasus is an attack blade with an unusual amount of stamina for its type: 60 attack, 30 stamina, 25 defense. In tournaments it is a steady presence rather than a dominant one: the tables below show where it stands today.

## The best Aero Pegasus combos: Rush first

The bit choice is almost settled: **Rush** is on about half of Aero Pegasus's top-cut placements, far ahead of Kick and Low Rush. What players still debate is the ratchet:

- **1-60 Rush** is the best-rated build overall.
- **1-50 Rush** sits even lower and is the main alternative; compare the two in the last-30-days column below.
- **7-60 Rush** is the usual third option.

Unlike Shark Scale, which has a clear favorite build, Aero Pegasus spreads its results across several ratchets. Pick the one you have: the gap between them is smaller than the gap between Rush and the other bits.

## The Level build from the World Championship

Aero Pegasus 7-60 Level was in the gold medalist's deck (Leobardo) at the 2025 World Championship, next to Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa and Shark Scale 3-60 Low Rush. It places less often than the Rush builds, but it is the version that won the biggest event.

## How it fits against the meta

Aero Pegasus faces the same two problems as every attacker: the stamina of [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) and the defense of [Wyvern Hover](/parts/hover-wyvern/). Its extra stamina is what separates it from [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/): it doesn't have to win the first clash to stay in the match, which makes it a forgiving choice for players moving into competitive attack builds.

## Where to get Aero Pegasus

Aero Pegasus is a Takara Tomy release, sold as Aero Pegasus 3-70A. The Rush bit and the 1-60 or 1-50 ratchets come from other sets, so plan on buying more than one product for a tournament build.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Aero Pegasus combo?** By tournament score, Aero Pegasus 1-60 Rush. If you want the version that won the biggest event, 7-60 Level was in the 2025 world champion's deck.

**Is Aero Pegasus good in tournaments?** Yes: it is one of the most-played attack blades of the past year, behind Shark Scale. It is a solid second attacker rather than the default first pick.

**Rush or Kick on Aero Pegasus?** Rush, by a wide margin. Kick and Low Rush are the usual second choices, and they place far less often.
