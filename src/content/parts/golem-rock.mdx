Golem Rock, sold by Hasbro as Rock Golem, is a defense blade: 60 defense, 30 attack, 10 stamina. It shows up in tournament results regularly, but it is the clearest case on this site of a blade without a settled build. Its placements are spread over many different combinations, and none of them has a high score.

That makes this a different kind of page: rather than one build to copy, here is what players have been trying.

## The Golem Rock builds that place

- **Golem Rock 9-60 Free Ball** is the best-rated build, and the 9-60 ratchet is the most common one under this blade.
- **Golem Rock 1-60 Hexa** and **7-60 Hexa** pair it with a stamina bit.
- **9-60 Low Orb**, **9-60 Wedge** and **1-60 Under Needle** complete the list.

No bit holds even a quarter of its top-cut placements. When the bit is that open, it usually means the blade gets picked for a matchup, not as a default.

## Golem Rock or Wyvern Hover?

If you want a defense blade for tournaments, [Wyvern Hover](/parts/hover-wyvern/) has the much stronger record, with a clear reference build (9-60 Kick). Golem Rock is the alternative to try when your deck already has Wyvern Hover, or the blade to experiment with if you like defense setups nobody else runs. Its 10 stamina also means it can't win a long match against [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) or [Silver Wolf](/parts/silver-wolf/).

## Where to get Golem Rock

Golem Rock debuted in the Takara Tomy booster UX-13 (Golem Rock 1-60UN), and it also came in the CX-11 Emperor Might Deck Set. Hasbro sells it as Rock Golem, for example in the G1676 starter pack. Both the UX-13 booster and the Hasbro starter come with the Under Needle bit, so 1-60 Under Needle is the build you can play straight out of the box.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Golem Rock combo?** Golem Rock 9-60 Free Ball has the best score, but the margin over the other builds is small, and the score itself is low compared with the top blades.

**Is Golem Rock good for tournaments?** It places, but rarely wins big events. Treat it as a second defense blade or a matchup pick, not as the centre of a deck.

**Is Golem Rock the same as Rock Golem?** Yes: Golem Rock is the Takara Tomy name, Rock Golem the Hasbro one.
