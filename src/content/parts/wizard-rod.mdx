Wizard Rod is the reference stamina blade of Beyblade X, sold by Hasbro as Wand Wizard. Its profile is lopsided on purpose: 60 stamina against 15 attack and 25 defense. It doesn't try to win clashes. It tries to still be spinning when the other bey stops, and in tournaments that plan works more often than almost anything else. The tables below show how often, recalculated every day.

## The best Wizard Rod combo: 1-60 Hexa

One build carries most of Wizard Rod's results: [Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa](/combos/wizard-rod-1-60-hexa/), which has held the top of this site's ranking for months. The 1-60 ratchet keeps the blade low and the Hexa bit keeps it turning with little friction, so the whole combo is built around outlasting.

The clearest proof is the 2025 World Championship podium: Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa was in both the gold medalist's deck (Leobardo) and the silver medalist's (Balya), and the bronze medalist (Berguiny) played Wizard Rod too, on 9-70 Ball ([decks as reported by Mall of Toys](https://malloftoys.com/blogs/news/beyblade-x-world-championship-2025-winners-and-winning-combos)). When the same blade sits in all three podium decks, it is setting the format.

## Other Wizard Rod builds worth knowing

If you don't have Hexa, the next builds in the ranking change the bit before the ratchet:

- **1-60 Free Ball** keeps the low ratchet and swaps to a ball tip that moves a little more.
- **9-60 Free Ball** and **9-60 Ball** raise the blade on a nine-sided ratchet, a common choice when 1-60 isn't available.
- **9-60 Hexa** and **7-60 Hexa** keep the bit and change the ratchet. They place far less often than 1-60 Hexa.

The pattern in the data is simple: the farther a build gets from 1-60 Hexa, the less it places. Most top-cut Wizard Rods run the 1-60 ratchet.

## What beats Wizard Rod

Pure stamina has one structural weakness: a bey that steals spin instead of out-spinning it. [Cobalt Dragoon](/parts/cobalt-dragoon/) spins left, and in its [5-60 Elevate build](/combos/cobalt-dragoon-5-60-elevate/) it erodes a right-spin stamina bey rather than trying to outlast it. It was in the same silver-medal deck as Wizard Rod, which tells you how players plan around the matchup.

The other answer is speed. [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/) aims to knock Wizard Rod out before stamina matters, and the two blades sit together at the top of the ranking as opposite ways to win.

## Where to get Wizard Rod

Wizard Rod debuted in the [UX-03 booster](/buy/UX-03/) as Wizard Rod 5-70DB, and it also came in Random Booster Vol. 4 (BX-35) and in the Hasbro Wand Wizard starter packs G1537 and G1538. BX-35 and G1538 sell it as Wizard Rod 1-60R, so they already include the 1-60 ratchet; the Hexa bit has to come from another set.
