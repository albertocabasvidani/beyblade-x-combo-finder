Silver Wolf, sold by Hasbro as Sterling Wolf, is the second big stamina blade of the Beyblade X meta after Wizard Rod. Its stats lean even further toward endurance: 65 stamina, 30 defense, 15 attack.

## The best Silver Wolf combo: 9-60 Free Ball

Silver Wolf's results concentrate on one build: **Silver Wolf 9-60 Free Ball**. The 9-60 ratchet is on more than half of its top-cut placements, and **Free Ball** is its most common bit.

The alternatives keep one of the two parts:

- **9-60 Low Orb** and **9-60 Ball** keep the ratchet and change the round-tipped bit.
- **9-70 Free Ball** keeps the bit on a taller ratchet.
- **9-60 Hexa** borrows Wizard Rod's favorite bit, with much weaker results on this blade.

## Silver Wolf or Wizard Rod?

Both are stamina blades, but they settle on different builds: [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) runs low on 1-60 Hexa, Silver Wolf sits on 9-60 Free Ball. Wizard Rod has the stronger tournament record, which is why most decks pick it first. Silver Wolf is the natural second stamina option, or the alternative when Wizard Rod is already in your deck and you need another endurance bey.

The threats are the same for both: a left-spin stealer like [Cobalt Dragoon](/parts/cobalt-dragoon/), and attackers like [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/) that try to end the match before stamina counts.

## Where to get Silver Wolf

Silver Wolf debuted in the Takara Tomy starter UX-08 (Silver Wolf 3-80FB). Hasbro sells it as Sterling Wolf in the G1674 starter pack, also with 3-80FB. Both already include the Free Ball bit: only the 9-60 ratchet has to come from another product. That makes Silver Wolf one of the simplest blades on this site to turn into its reference tournament build.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Silver Wolf combo?** Silver Wolf 9-60 Free Ball, well ahead of every other build.

**Is Silver Wolf good?** Yes, as a stamina blade. It places regularly, though Wizard Rod is the stronger choice when you can only bring one stamina bey.

**Is Sterling Wolf the same as Silver Wolf?** Yes: Silver Wolf is the Takara Tomy name, Sterling Wolf the Hasbro one.
