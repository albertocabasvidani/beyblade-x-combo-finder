Meteor Dragoon has the highest attack stat on this list: 75 attack, with 35 stamina and 15 defense. It is a newer release than most of the top blades, and it has already built a tournament record of its own.

## The best Meteor Dragoon combos: Level on 7-60 and 7-70

Where most attackers run Rush or Low Rush, Meteor Dragoon's results go to a different bit: **Level** is on the large majority of its top-cut placements. The ratchet is almost always a seven-sided one:

- **Meteor Dragoon 7-60 Level** is the best-rated build.
- **Meteor Dragoon 7-70 Level** is the taller version, close behind.
- **Meteor Dragoon 9-60 Elevate** is the main alternative bit, the same Elevate that defines [Cobalt Dragoon](/parts/cobalt-dragoon/)'s builds.

Builds with Rush or Low Rush exist, but they barely register in the results: this blade has found its own setup.

## Meteor Dragoon against the other attackers

With more attack than [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/) and more stamina than [Phoenix Wing](/parts/phoenix-wing/), Meteor Dragoon looks strong on paper, and its record says it is a real option. It still trails the most-played attackers in volume, partly because it reached players later.

Its matchups are those of every attacker: the stamina of [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) and the defense of [Wyvern Hover](/parts/hover-wyvern/). Check the last-30-days column below to see whether it is gaining ground.

## Where to get Meteor Dragoon

Meteor Dragoon came out in the Takara Tomy starter UX-17 (Meteor Dragoon 3-70J). The Level bit and the 7-60 ratchet of its tournament builds come from other products, so the starter is a way to get the blade, not a ready tournament bey.

## Why Level instead of Rush?

The data doesn't explain the reason, but it shows the pattern clearly: on Meteor Dragoon, the Rush family of bits that defines Shark Scale and Phoenix Wing barely places, while Level carries the results. If you copy a build from another attacker onto this blade, you are likely to get a weaker bey than the one players actually bring to tournaments.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Meteor Dragoon combo?** Meteor Dragoon 7-60 Level, with 7-70 Level close behind.

**Is Meteor Dragoon better than Shark Scale?** It has more attack on paper, but Shark Scale has a far larger tournament record. Meteor Dragoon is the newer challenger, not yet the default.
