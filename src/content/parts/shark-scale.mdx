Shark Scale, sold by Hasbro as Scale Shark, is the most-played attack blade in Beyblade X tournaments over the past year. Its stats leave no doubt about the plan: 70 attack, 15 defense, 15 stamina. It has to hit first and finish the match early, because it has almost nothing in reserve if the match goes long. The tables below count how often that works, updated every day.

## The best Shark Scale combos: Low Rush on 1-70 and 3-60

Shark Scale's results come mostly from two builds that share the same bit:

- [**Shark Scale 1-70 Low Rush**](/combos/shark-scale-1-70-low-rush/) is the most-played version. It was in the silver medalist's deck (Balya) at the 2025 World Championship.
- **Shark Scale 3-60 Low Rush** was in the gold medalist's deck (Leobardo) at the same event.

Low Rush is the common thread: it is the bit on almost half of Shark Scale's top-cut placements. It gives a fast, low attack line, which suits a blade that wins by impact. The ratchet is where players disagree: 1-70 and 3-60 split the top results, with 1-60 a step behind.

## Rush and the other variants

Swapping Low Rush for **Rush** gives the next group of builds (3-60 Rush, 1-60 Rush). They place less often, but they are the usual fallback when Low Rush isn't in your collection. The 9-60 ratchet and Free Ball or Kick bits appear too, mostly as experiments rather than staples.

## What stops Shark Scale

Two very different blades answer it:

- [**Wizard Rod**](/parts/wizard-rod/) avoids the clash and wins on stamina. If Shark Scale doesn't finish the match quickly, Wizard Rod is still spinning when it stops.
- [**Wyvern Hover**](/parts/hover-wyvern/) (Hover Wyvern for Hasbro) takes the hit instead. Its [9-60 Kick build](/combos/hover-wyvern-9-60-kick/) is built to absorb repeated attacks.

With 15 defense, Shark Scale rarely survives a long exchange: against both answers it has to land the decisive hit in the first moments of the match.

## Where to get Shark Scale

Shark Scale came out in the UX-15 SharkScale Deck Set, as Shark Scale 4-50UF, and Hasbro sells it in the G2731 booster as Scale Shark. Neither includes the Low Rush bit or the 1-70 ratchet, so a tournament build needs parts from other sets.
