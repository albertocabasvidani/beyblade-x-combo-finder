Phoenix Wing, sold by Hasbro as Soar Phoenix, is one of the early Beyblade X attack blades that never left competitive play. It has 60 attack, 25 defense and 15 stamina, and it still reaches top cuts more than most of the newer releases, even if it is no longer the attacker players reach for first.

## The best Phoenix Wing combos: Rush on 3-60

**Rush** is the main bit, on close to half of Phoenix Wing's top-cut placements, with **Low Rush** as the second choice. The ratchet that comes up most is **3-60**:

- **Phoenix Wing 3-60 Rush** is the best-rated build.
- **Phoenix Wing 1-60 Rush** is the lower alternative, with a similar record.
- **Phoenix Wing 3-60 Low Rush** is the main alternative bit on the same ratchet.

The 9-60 and 7-60 ratchets complete the picture: they place, but less often.

## Phoenix Wing against the other attackers

Next to [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/), the most-played attack blade, and [Aero Pegasus](/parts/aero-pegasus/), Phoenix Wing is the older option with a slightly more balanced profile: 25 defense instead of Shark Scale's 15. Its builds look the same as theirs (low ratchet, Rush-family bit), so the choice between them often comes down to which blade you own.

Like every attacker, it has to deal with [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/)'s stamina and [Wyvern Hover](/parts/hover-wyvern/)'s defense, and the tables below show how it holds up today.

## Where to get Phoenix Wing

It debuted in the Takara Tomy starter BX-23 (Phoenix Wing 9-60GF) and also came in Random Booster Vol. 4 (BX-35) as Phoenix Wing 5-80H. Hasbro sells it as Soar Phoenix, for example in the G1756 booster. None of these releases includes the Rush bit, so the competitive builds need one more product.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Phoenix Wing combo?** Phoenix Wing 3-60 Rush has the best tournament score, with 1-60 Rush as the close alternative.

**Is Phoenix Wing still good in Beyblade X?** Yes. It is no longer the first attacker players choose, but it keeps reaching top cuts, and its builds use the same parts as the newer attack blades.

**Is Soar Phoenix the same as Phoenix Wing?** Yes: Phoenix Wing is the Takara Tomy name, Soar Phoenix the Hasbro one.
