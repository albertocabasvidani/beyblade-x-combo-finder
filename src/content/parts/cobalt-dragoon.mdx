Cobalt Dragoon is the left-spin blade of the Beyblade X meta, and that one fact explains its whole role. A left-spin bey meeting a right-spin bey doesn't just collide with it: it can steal its rotation. Against the stamina blades that sit at the top of the ranking, that is a different weapon from raw attack or raw endurance.

On paper it is an attack blade, with 60 attack, 25 stamina and 15 defense. In practice, players build it to take spin from its opponents.

## The best Cobalt Dragoon combos: Elevate on 5-60 or 9-60

No other blade on this list is as settled on a single bit: **Elevate** is on the large majority of Cobalt Dragoon's top-cut placements. The ratchet is the only real decision:

- [**Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate**](/combos/cobalt-dragoon-5-60-elevate/) is the reference build. It was in the silver medalist's deck (Balya) at the 2025 World Championship, next to Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa and Shark Scale 1-70 Low Rush.
- **Cobalt Dragoon 9-60 Elevate** is close behind; the last-30-days column below shows which of the two is placing more right now.

Everything else, from 1-60 Elevate to the Level bit, places far less often.

## Why it counters Wizard Rod

[Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) wins by outlasting. Cobalt Dragoon doesn't try to outlast it: it erodes its spin through contact in the opposite direction. That's why a deck built around Wizard Rod often carries a plan for Cobalt Dragoon, and why Balya's silver-medal deck ran both. The [Wizard Rod page](/parts/wizard-rod/) covers the other side of the matchup.

## Weak spots

With 15 defense, Cobalt Dragoon has little to offer against a dedicated attacker like [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/) that aims to finish the match on the first impact. Its value grows with the share of stamina beys in your local meta.

## Where to get Cobalt Dragoon

Cobalt Dragoon debuted in the Takara Tomy starter BX-34 (Cobalt Dragoon 2-60C) and also came in Random Booster Vol. 7 (CX-08) as Cobalt Dragoon 4-55WB. Hasbro uses the same name. Neither version includes Elevate, so the tournament build needs the bit from another product.

## Quick answers

**What is the best Cobalt Dragoon combo?** Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate, with 9-60 Elevate as a near equal. Without Elevate, the blade loses most of what makes it a tournament pick.

**Is Cobalt Dragoon an attack or a stamina blade?** Its stats say attack, but its job in a deck is spin stealing: it is there to beat right-spin stamina beys, Wizard Rod above all.
