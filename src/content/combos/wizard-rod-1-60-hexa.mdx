Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa isn't a combo that wins on points: it wins because it never ends. Score 9.62/10, the highest recorded on this site — and not from a single inflated pillar: performance 0.986, presence 0.962, corroboration 0.88. Three numbers all near the maximum at once are rare; usually a combo either dominates the results, or is everywhere on the brackets, or has a large enough sample to be sure — rarely all three together.

The raw data confirms it: 1,279 tournament placements, 709 wins, 2,274 top cut appearances, 14% meta share in the last month. It runs on Xtreme and Infinity stadiums without any notable difference — it isn't a combo tuned for a single surface.

## Why it dominates

The build is the textbook definition of pure stamina: Wizard Rod (atk15/def25/sta60) on the long 1-60 axis with the Hexa bit, round and low-friction. It doesn't attack, it doesn't actively defend — it outlasts almost everything that comes against it, and in a format where spin time decides the spin finishes, outlasting is almost always winning.

The data point that closes the discussion is September 19, 2026, at Mata Nui's X Extravaganza 2 on an Xtreme stadium: a **full podium**, 1st-2nd-3rd place in the same tournament. It isn't half a dozen scattered placements across different months — it's three different players reaching the same conclusion at the same event.

And it is the combo of both finalists (gold and silver) at the 2025 World Championship. On the WBO all-time ranking it is #1, with 3,087 points and 900 wins according to that scoring system — a different number from this site's 709, because the evidence windows and criteria are different: they aren't directly comparable, but they point in the same direction.

## Who really beats it

There is only one reliable answer in pure endurance, and it has to be said without beating around the bush: [Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate](/combos/cobalt-dragoon-5-60-elevate/), score 8.68. It isn't a direct stamina rival — it's a left-spin stealer. It steals spin through opposite-spin erosion instead of trying to last longer in a symmetric way. It's a different mechanism, not a superiority of build, and that makes it the only structural threat that Wizard Rod can't simply "outlast".

The signal to watch isn't the score, it's adoption: from 8 to 24 placements between July and August 2026, almost tripled. Players are picking it deliberately as a counter to this combo, and that trend isn't statistical noise — it's a real-time meta response.

## The attack challenge

[Shark Scale 1-70 Low Rush](/combos/shark-scale-1-70-low-rush/) tries the opposite road: closing the match before endurance matters, instead of competing on duration. Score 8.93, #2 WBO all-time, used by silver medalist Balya at Worlds 2025 — it isn't a fallback build. But "before endurance matters" is the fragile part of the plan: if the rush doesn't land in the first phase of the exchange, the match goes back to being a stamina race, and that's the race Wizard Rod almost always wins.

## How to get the parts

Here the winning build doesn't match the factory configuration. The [Wizard Rod](/parts/wizard-rod/) blade is found in the [UX-03 Booster](/buy/UX-03/) set, but that set pairs it by default with a 5-70DB ratchet and bit — an attack/defense configuration, not a stamina one. To get to 1-60 Hexa you need to buy UX-03 for the blade and then source the 1-60 ratchet and the Hexa bit separately, from other sets that include them in their native configuration.
