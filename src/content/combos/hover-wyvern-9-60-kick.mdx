Hover Wyvern is a defense blade (atk 13 / def 60 / sta 27), and on Xtreme the combination with the 9-60 ratchet and the Kick bit turns it into something different from the usual defender that sits in the center and waits. Kick gives balanced movement: it isn't the aggressive mobility of an attacker, but it is enough to take the combo out of the direct path of whoever charges. A 60-point defense that stayed still would become an easy target to surround — Kick closes that gap while keeping the ability to absorb direct hits intact. The result is a combo that holds 60 defense without paying the typical price, immobility.

The numbers confirm it: score 8.55/10, 451 tournament events recorded, 173 wins, 551 top cut appearances, 2.2% meta share in the last month. It isn't an emerging phenomenon, it's a stable and widespread presence in the competitive meta.

## Recent results and community verdict

On the field, [Hover Wyvern](/parts/hover-wyvern/) placed 1st and 2nd at the Bi-Weekly BBX Tournament #3 - Beybladin' X Spokane on September 1, 2026, with 30 players. Two and a half weeks later, on September 19, 2026, it took 3rd place at the Beycon 26 Dallas Regionals with 31 players — two different events, two different weekends, two fields of comparable size. Not an isolated hit: repeated performances in separate competitive contexts.

The community verdict converges in the same direction. The PXD tier list ranks Hover Wyvern as an X-tier defender, the highest grade available. And in the WBO all-time ranking — a different scoring system from ours, so not comparable number for number — the combo holds the #7 spot overall, with 355 points and 185 wins according to that ranking. Three independent sources (tournaments, tier list, historical ranking) telling the same story.

## The matchup against Shark Scale

The main target of this combo in the current meta is [Shark Scale](/parts/shark-scale/), the most played attack blade in the game. In its reference build, [Shark Scale 1-70 Low Rush](/combos/shark-scale-1-70-low-rush/) (score 8.93, #2 in the WBO all-time ranking), the goal is clear: fast KO, constant pressure, a quick close to the match before the opponent can react.

Against a profile like that, a pure, immobile defense risks being surrounded, which is exactly what Kick is designed to avoid. Hover Wyvern 9-60 Kick answers in the structurally correct way: it absorbs the impact thanks to the 60 defense points of the ratchet, and uses Kick's mobility to avoid offering a fixed target to an attacker aiming at exactly that. It is the category answer to a play style that eliminates most of the field through sheer speed of resolution.

## Two philosophies of survival

The comparison with [Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa](/combos/wizard-rod-1-60-hexa/) — score 9.62, #1 overall in the WBO all-time ranking — brings into focus two opposite ways of not losing. Wizard Rod wins through stamina and pure duration: it stays in play longer than anyone else, and the match is decided by the rival's exhaustion. Hover Wyvern wins by absorbing hits: it doesn't outlast the opponent, it neutralizes them by taking the impact and still staying in motion.

They are two survival logics that don't replace each other, and the fact that both combos are steadily at the top of the meta says that the game rewards more than one path to the same result: not losing.
