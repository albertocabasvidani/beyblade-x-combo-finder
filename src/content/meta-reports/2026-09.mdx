By September 19th, 161 tournament placements are logged for the month. On its own that number means nothing — a month is only 19 days in, and the honest question is whether 161 is low, on pace, or ahead. Answering it means comparing against how the same first-19-days window looked in previous months, not against the eventual full-month total.

## Why this report treats September's numbers as incomplete

Across May, June, July and August 2026, the placements counted by day 19 ranged from 42% to 84% of that month's eventual full-month total — averaging around 60%. Applied to September, that pattern would put the expected count by day 19 somewhere between roughly 600 and 1,000, extrapolating from August's own 1,203-placement month. What's actually logged is 161 — far below every one of those four months' pace, not just below the average.

That gap is almost certainly reporting lag on the WBO forum threads this site scrapes, not a real 85%+ drop in tournament activity. Beyblade X tournaments don't stop happening because a report is being written; results get posted to the forum with a delay that varies week to week, and September's posting appears to be running well behind where August's was at the same point in its month. Treat everything below as a snapshot of what's visible right now, not a claim about what actually happened on the ground this month — the two are not the same thing in September's data, whatever they are in a completed month like August.

## What's leading, for what it's worth

With that caveat attached, the current placements-so-far ranking has [Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa](/combos/wizard-rod-1-60-hexa/) on top with 14, followed by Silver Wolf 9-60 Free Ball with 7, Wizard Rod 1-60 Free Ball with 5, [Shark Scale 1-70 Low Rush](/combos/shark-scale-1-70-low-rush/) with 5, and Shark Scale 3-60 Low Rush with 3. That's the same top names that led August's full month — Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa's position at #1 is consistent with a combo that just posted a full podium sweep (1st-2nd-3rd) at Mata Nui's X Extravaganza 2 on the 19th itself, so at least that entry looks like signal rather than an artifact of incomplete data.

What this early count can't tell us is whether August's risers — [Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate](/combos/cobalt-dragoon-5-60-elevate/), which nearly tripled its placements last month, and the two Shark Scale Low Rush builds — are still climbing, holding, or cooling off. Their September numbers so far are too thin against too short a window to say anything, and comparing them to August's full-month totals directly would repeat the exact mistake this report is trying to avoid.

That mistake is worth spelling out with the clearest example in this month's data. Wizard Rod 1-60 Hexa logged 125 placements in all of August; so far in September it's at 14. Read naively, that looks like a combo in freefall — a 90% drop for the site's own #1-scored build. It isn't one: 14 placements by day 19 of a month that historically lands 42-84% of its total by that point isn't a decline, it's the same lag affecting every combo in this report, landing hardest on whichever combo had the most placements to lose. [Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate](/combos/cobalt-dragoon-5-60-elevate/) shows the identical pattern at smaller scale, 24 placements in August against 3 so far in September. Neither number says anything about September's actual meta yet.

## What next month's report will actually be able to say

Once September closes and the lag catches up, next month's report will compare a complete month against a complete month — the only comparison that's fair. Until then, the one thing worth carrying forward is the historical day-19 share itself: if a future month's early count looks unusually low or high against that 42-84% range again, that's the signal to check for lag before reading it as a trend, not the other way around.
